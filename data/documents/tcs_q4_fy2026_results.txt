Tata Consultancy Services Announces Results for Fourth Quarter and Full Year Ended March 31, 2026

MUMBAI, April 15, 2026 — Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) (BSE: 532540, NSE: TCS) today reported its consolidated financial results for the fourth quarter and full year ended March 31, 2026.

Q4 FY2026 Financial Highlights:
- Revenue: ₹6,82,340 crore (USD 81.6 billion), up 3.2% QoQ
- EBITDA: ₹1,84,232 crore, margin at 27.0%
- Net Income: ₹1,26,432 crore, up 2.1% QoQ
- Operating Cash Flow: ₹1,42,876 crore, 113% of Net Income
- Earnings Per Share: ₹34.92

Full Year FY2026 Highlights:
- Revenue: ₹26,70,210 crore, up 4.5% YoY
- EBITDA: ₹7,22,740 crore, margin at 27.1%
- Net Income: ₹4,92,100 crore, up 1.3% YoY
- Total Dividend per share: ₹58 (including special dividend of ₹10)
- ROCE: 54.9%

Key Business Highlights:
- BFSI vertical grew 5.1% YoY, led by strong demand in North America
- Retail & CPG grew 4.2% YoY
- Life Sciences & Healthcare grew 6.8% YoY
- North America revenue grew 3.8% YoY; UK grew 5.2% YoY
- Continental Europe grew 2.1% YoY
- Asia Pacific grew 4.5% YoY
- Total workforce: 6,21,721 as of March 31, 2026
- Net addition of 3,412 employees during Q4
- IT services attrition at 12.3% (LTM)
- Women in workforce: 35.6%
- Nationalities represented: 153
- Generative AI pipeline grew 3x YoY; 450+ active projects
- Won 3 large deals with total contract value > USD 50 million each
- Signed strategic partnership with a leading European bank for cloud transformation

Management Commentary:
K Krithivasan, Chief Executive Officer & Managing Director, said: "We closed FY26 on a strong note, with broad-based growth across all our verticals and geographies. Our investments in AI and cloud capabilities are yielding tangible results, reflected in the 3x growth of our GenAI pipeline. We remain focused on helping our clients navigate their transformation journeys."

Samir Seksaria, Chief Financial Officer, said: "Our disciplined execution resulted in industry-leading operating margins and strong cash conversion of 113%. The board's recommendation of a special dividend reflects our confidence in the business momentum and our commitment to shareholder returns."

Outlook for FY2027:
- Management expects demand environment to improve gradually
- Capital expenditure guidance: ₹31,000 crore for FY2027
- Focus on building AI talent: plan to train 25,000 employees on GenAI
- Expect continued margins in 26-28% range
- Dividend policy remains progressive

About TCS:
Tata Consultancy Services is an IT services, consulting and business solutions organization that has been partnering with many of the world's largest businesses in their transformation journeys for over 50 years. TCS offers a consulting-led, cognitive powered, integrated portfolio of business, technology and engineering services and solutions.
