# MOSI Report: Granules India
**Published**: 2026-08-01

## 1. Business Model
Granules operates as a vertically integrated pharmaceutical manufacturing company focused on core APIs, PFIs, and Finished Dosages. Its core strategy relies on high volume, low margin scale economies.

### Products and Segments
The company's primary products include Paracetamol, Ibuprofen, and Metformin. It serves two main customer segments: B2B API Supply and US Generic Rx.

### Manufacturing Plants
Granules operates several facilities, notably:
- Vizag Block 4 API Facility (Capacity: 2,500 MT, Status: Commissioned)
- Gagillapur PFI Facility
- Bonthapally Facility

## 2. Financial Performance & Margins
Revenue CAGR has been 24% over the last five fiscal years. Operating margins have steadily improved due to backward integration.

## 3. Management Quality & Philosophy
### Capital Allocation
Prudent allocation focused on core APIs. The company does not chase unrelated diversifications.

### Execution Track Record
Consistent track record of delivering margins over the last 10 years, despite pricing pressure in the US.

### Forward Guidance
Management has explicitly stated they are expected to grow at 20%+ CAGR for the next three years.

### Communication Transparency
High transparency, clear disclosures in quarterly results.

### Governance and Board
Independent board with strong pedigree.

### Promoter Skin In Game
High promoter holding with zero pledge, demonstrating strong conviction.
