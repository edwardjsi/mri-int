## Executive Summary

### The Problem

Indian financial advisors are in a structural bind. Client AUM is growing — Demat accounts crossed 160 million in 2025, SIP inflows consistently exceed ₹20,000 crore per month — but the tools available to India's wealth management professionals have not kept pace. Most solo RIAs and boutique wealth managers operate with 1–3 analysts, manage 50–200 client portfolios, and lack the quant infrastructure that institutional desks take for granted. They are expected to generate alpha, comply with SEBI's tightened Investment Adviser regulations, maintain auditable research trails for every recommendation, and communicate portfolio performance to clients at scale — all with spreadsheet-based processes and generic news terminals.

Meanwhile, the tools that do exist are built for US markets, priced in USD, and ill-suited to Indian NSE equities. Bloomberg, FactSet, and Koyfin offer institutional-grade quant analysis, but at price points and data models that are irrelevant for a solo RIA in Mumbai managing ₹200 crore in AUM. The result is a widening information asymmetry: institutional quant desks at domestic and foreign institutions have machine-generated re-rating signals, regime classification, and earnings-debate forensics — while the advisor managing retail client money relies on gut feel, trailing financials, and a Bloomberg terminal he cannot afford.

SEBI's tightening of Investment Adviser regulations (suitability norms, documentation requirements, client-level risk profiling mandates) has raised the stakes further. Every buy recommendation now requires a defensible research process. Every sell requires an audit trail. Advisors who cannot produce that trail face regulatory risk. The problem is not just commercial — it is compliance exposure.

### The Solution

Market Regime Intelligence (MRI) is a daily quantitative decision-support platform purpose-built for Indian equities (NSE/BSE listed). It delivers four integrated outputs on a daily cadence:

1. **Market Regime Classification** — a breadth-based Risk-On / Neutral / Risk-Off signal that determines whether the market environment is hospitable to momentum strategies or requires capital preservation. Advisors know, every morning, whether they should be hunting setups or protecting capital.

2. **Stock Scoring Engine** — a 0–100 composite score across five weighted factors (Trend Integrity 25%, Trend Slope 25%, Relative Strength 20%, Price Proximity 20%, Volume Surge 10%) applied daily across 800+ Nifty 500 and BSE Group A stocks. Advisors have a ranked universe at their fingertips every morning.

3. **Structural Quality Filters** — six structural signals (Margin Quality, TAM Expansion, Backward Integration, Forward Integration, Moat Strengthening, Geographic Expansion) validated against financial time series (sales CAGR, EBITDA margins, ROCE, working capital cycles, FCF conversion) to distinguish re-rating candidates from value traps. The "Most systems find 'good businesses.' MRI finds *Re-rating Conditions*" differentiator.

4. **Forensic AI Debate** — an LLM-driven layer that reads every earnings transcript, tracks management tone across nine dimensions (Confidence, Hedging, Aggression, Transparency, Optimism, Pessimism, Accountability, Numerical Density), detects tone shifts quarter-over-quarter, and verifies management promises against actual financial outcomes. An advisor can surface a "Tone Shift" alert before the change shows up in the numbers.

MRI does not execute trades. It does not generate buy/sell signals. It provides institutional-grade research artifacts — daily reports, regime signals, scoring dashboards, and AI forensics — that advisors use to build defensible investment processes, satisfy SEBI documentation requirements, and communicate research conclusions to clients with confidence.

## Market Context & ICP

### 2.1 Market Context — Why Now

#### Indian RIA Market Growth

SEBI has registered over 1,200 Investment Advisers (RIAs) as of 2025, a number that has grown steadily as the regulator has professionalized the category. Total RIA AUM is estimated at approximately ₹4–5 lakh crore and growing at 25–30% YoY. More significantly, the composition of RIA AUM is shifting: the share of fee-only, non-commission AUM is increasing as clients become more fee-aware and SEBI's distributor-remuneration regulations pressure the commission-based model. Fee-only RIAs are the highest-quality MRI prospects — they have no product-selling conflict, their revenue depends on performance and trust, and they have the strongest need for defensible research processes.

#### Retail Equity AUM Boom

India now has over 16 crore (160 million) active Demat accounts, a 10x increase over the past decade. Monthly SIP inflows consistently exceed ₹20,000 crore. This is the largest retail equity investor base in India's history, and it is being managed overwhelmingly by advisors, RIAs, and wealth managers who are overwhelmed by the research burden. Every new client account means more portfolios to monitor, more recommendations to justify, and more documentation to maintain.

#### Regulatory Tailwinds

SEBI's Investment Adviser regulations (IA Regulations, 2013, as amended) require RIAs to maintain detailed records of investment advice given, suitability assessments, and client risk profiling. SEBI's tightened suitability norms (2020–2024 rounds) have raised the documentation bar further. Every buy/sell recommendation must be traceable to a documented research process. This creates a structural demand for research artifacts — regime views, scoring frameworks, fundamental analysis, earnings forensics — that advisors can point to when SEBI audits their practice. MRI directly addresses this compliance burden by delivering daily research artifacts that form an auditable trail.

#### The Competitive Gap

The quant tools available to Indian advisors are either:
- **Global tools (Bloomberg, FactSet, Koyfin):** USD-priced, US-markets-primary, no NSE-specific data, overkill for a solo RIA, no compliance framing.
- **Domestic platforms (Valueresearch, Tick, Capital Mind):** Primarily retail-oriented, no regime engine, no structural signals, no earnings forensics at the depth MRI offers.
- **Excel + broker terminal:** The default for most RIAs. Manual, error-prone, unscalable, not auditable.

MRI occupies a specific white space: institutional-grade quant analysis for Indian equities, at advisor-affordable INR pricing, with compliance-friendly output framing. There is no direct competitor in this position today.

### 2.3 Top 5 Advisor Pain Points (Mapped to MRI Capabilities)

#### Pain Point 1: Compliance Documentation Burden
**The problem:** SEBI IA regulations require advisors to maintain auditable records of every investment recommendation — the research basis, suitability assessment, and rationale. Most RIAs store this in email threads and Excel sheets. When SEBI audits, the documentation is either missing or thin. The risk is regulatory: warning letters, suspension, or de-registration.

**MRI response:** MRI's daily reports, regime classifications, stock scoring outputs, and AI forensic analysis are SEBI-defensible research artifacts. An advisor can show SEBI: "On [date], our MRI regime engine flagged Risk-Off. We reduced equity exposure accordingly. The scoring on [stock] was 45/100 — below our 75-point threshold for new buys. This is our documented research process." MRI does not provide legal advice on compliance; advisors should consult their own legal counsel. But the research artifacts MRI produces create an objective, timestamped, auditable trail.

#### Pain Point 2: Scaling Research Without Headcount
**The problem:** An advisor managing 100 client portfolios across 50–100 stocks cannot manually track every name. Research is the bottleneck. Most advisors focus on 10–20 names they know well and neglect the rest of their universe. Coverage gaps create risk and missed opportunities.

**MRI response:** MRI runs the scoring engine across 800+ stocks every morning. The advisor gets a ranked universe, a regime view, and a structural signal summary — daily — without lifting a finger. The Master Investor Checklist quality scoring, the six structural signals, and the macro correlation engine cover what a team of analysts would take days to produce. A solo RIA with 150 client portfolios gets institutional-quality daily research coverage at zero marginal headcount cost.

#### Pain Point 3: Consistently Outperforming Nifty
**The problem:** Clients compare their advisor to the Nifty. Advisors who underperform in a bull market lose clients to index funds. Advisors who call a crash badly face the same outcome. The core need is alpha — not speculation, but a systematic edge grounded in data.

**MRI response:** MRI's 5-factor scoring system (Trend Integrity, Trend Slope, Relative Strength, Price Proximity, Volume Surge) is purpose-built to identify relative-strength setups before they become consensus. The regime engine prevents "buying the dip" in bear markets — the single biggest destroyer of advisor track records. The structural signals identify re-rating candidates before the market prices them in. *Backtested results: 26.39% CAGR vs. 10.5% Nifty 50 B&H over 5 years (2021–2026, walk-forward, including T+1 slippage). Backtested results are not indicative of future performance.*

#### Pain Point 4: Defending Underperformance
**The problem:** Every advisor has losing positions. When a client asks "why did we own this stock?", the answer matters for client retention and regulatory documentation. "I had a feeling" is not a defensible answer. Advisors need an objective, rules-based process they can point to — before the loss happens and after.

**MRI response:** MRI's scoring system is deterministic, rules-based, and fully transparent in its methodology. The 0–100 score is calculated from five weighted factors applied uniformly across the universe. The structural signals are validated against financial time series. The AI forensics layer documents management tone and promises. An advisor can show a client: "We bought this stock at a score of 82. The regime was Risk-On. It had a Margin Quality signal + TAM Expansion signal. When the score dropped below 60 and the regime shifted to Risk-Off, we initiated an Exit Review. Here is our documented process." This is not a guarantee of performance — it is a defensible process that builds client trust even in drawdowns.

#### Pain Point 5: Client Communication at Scale
**The problem:** High-touch client reviews are the single biggest time sink for wealth managers. Preparing quarterly review decks, writing portfolio commentary, explaining positioning rationale — each advisor spends 5–10 hours per client per year on client communication. At 100 clients, that is 500–1,000 hours per year.

**MRI response:** MRI's daily email digest and portfolio risk reports give advisors pre-built client-ready research materials. The regime view (Risk-On / Risk-Off / Neutral) is a simple, powerful narrative frame for client communication: "Our process says the market regime has shifted to Risk-Off. Here is what that means for your portfolio, and here is our response." The tone shift alerts and structural signal updates give advisors concrete, data-backed talking points for client calls. An advisor can walk into a quarterly review with the MRI daily digest in hand and have a full research brief ready in minutes.

## Pricing & Packaging

### 4.1 Pricing Philosophy

MRI is priced on value-based B2B logic, not cost-plus or usage-based metrics. The fundamental value proposition is institutional-grade research delivered without an in-house quant team — which means the return on a ₹4,999/month subscription is measured in advisor time saved, regulatory risk reduced, and client conversations elevated. A solo RIA managing ₹200 crore in AUM generates roughly ₹20–50 lakh per year in fee revenue; a ₹60,000 annual subscription is less than 3% of gross revenue and a fraction of the cost of a single junior analyst. MRI is priced to be a no-brainer at the solo tier and a compelling ROI story at the Practice and Enterprise tiers.

MRI is INR-priced specifically to eliminate FX friction and credit-card currency risk for Indian advisors. Most Indian wealth professionals do not hold USD credit cards or have corporate USD accounts; USD SaaS pricing is a documented conversion-killer for this segment. INR-native pricing removes every barrier between "interest" and "payment." The price floor is set materially above the retail tier (₹1,499–₹2,999/month) to signal B2B positioning, differentiate from the consumer-grade self-service market, and ensure the revenue per advisor customer can fund product development and support. Advisors who pay more engage more, renew at higher rates, and refer more often. Pricing at the right level is a signal, not just a revenue mechanism.

## Go-to-Market Plan

### 5.1 GTM Philosophy

MRI's go-to-market strategy is built for solo-founder execution at Indian advisor scale. Three principles drive every channel decision:

**Solo-founder capacity requires low-CAC, high-touch channels first.** Paid acquisition (LinkedIn ads, Google Ads) requires budget, creative testing, and iteration time that a single operator cannot sustain while also building product, running demos, and managing customers. The first 90 days are funded by Immanuel's time, not Immanuel's budget. Every channel selected here can be executed with 25–35 hours/week of focused effort — without hiring, contracting creative agencies, or managing campaign pixels.

**Advisors trust peer proof, regulatory-defensible research, and direct founder relationships more than ad copy.** This ICP is skeptical of salesmanship. A cold ad for a quant tool will be ignored; a LinkedIn post showing MRI's Risk-Off signal correctly calling the last market correction will get 50 connection requests. The channel strategy prioritizes "earn attention through evidence" over "buy attention through ads." Inbound leads from content outperform outbound leads in conversion rate for this reason.

**India-specific: LinkedIn-first because Indian advisors are concentrated there.** Twitter/X in India skews retail and finance-curious rather than SEBI-registered practitioner. WhatsApp groups are powerful distribution channels but are not indexable or scalable for a solo founder. LinkedIn is the professional network where RIAs, wealth managers, and PMS managers congregate, post about regulatory challenges, share market views, and form business relationships. It is the single highest-leverage channel for this ICP and geography.

### 5.4 Channel-Specific Compliance Guardrails

Every channel execution in this 90-day plan is subject to SEBI-aligned compliance rules. These are not optional — they protect Immanuel, protect MRI, and protect the advisor customers. Violations risk account restrictions (LinkedIn), regulatory scrutiny, and loss of advisor trust.

**Rule 1 — No performance promises in any channel.**
- LinkedIn posts: Never state "MRI helped advisors avoid the crash" or "stocks with MRI scores above 75 outperformed by X%." Framed historical data ("Our Risk-Off signal preceded the following market behavior...") is permitted with a mandatory trailing disclaimer: *"Backtested results are not indicative of future performance. MRI is a decision-support tool, not investment advice."*
- Blog posts: Same rule. Include the disclaimer in the article header and footer.
- Email/ newsletters: Same rule. Include disclaimer in footer.
- Partner content: Same rule. Both parties' disclaimers required.

**Rule 2 — LinkedIn profile and banner must include the compliance disclaimer.**
- Banner text: *"Decision-support analytics. Not investment advice. Backtested results are not indicative of future performance."*
- About section: "MRI provides quantitative research analytics for SEBI-registered advisors. It does not generate buy/sell recommendations or guaranteed returns."
- Every post with a chart, score, or data point: trailing disclaimer in comments or in the post body.

**Rule 3 — Email footer compliance.**
Every email sent through any channel must include:
- *"MRI is a quantitative decision-support tool. It does not provide investment advice, buy/sell recommendations, or guaranteed returns. Past backtested performance is not indicative of future results. SEBI disclaimer: MRI is not registered as an Investment Adviser with SEBI."*

**Rule 4 — No testimonials claiming returns.**
Peer testimonials are powerful — use them. But they must be qualitative, not performance-based:
- Permitted: "MRI reduced my morning research time from 90 minutes to 20 minutes." "The regime view gave me the confidence to explain my positioning to clients."
- Prohibited: "MRI helped me beat the market by X%." "My clients' portfolios outperformed since I started using MRI."

**Rule 5 — Affiliate disclosures required.**
Any partner-facing communication about the referral or revenue share program must state the revenue share percentage (20% recurring) explicitly and clearly. This is an SEBI alignment requirement and an ethical obligation.

**Rule 6 — No SEBI endorsement language.**
MRI is not SEBI-registered. No channel copy should imply, state, or imply by omission that SEBI has approved, endorsed, or is associated with MRI. If an advisor asks if MRI is SEBI-registered, the answer is: "No — MRI is a research analytics tool. We explicitly do not provide investment advice, and advisors should use MRI's outputs as part of their own research process, not as a substitute for it."

#### 8.3.1 North Star Metric

**Metric Name:** Paying Advisor Subscriptions (B2B tier)

**Definition:** A paying customer who has completed at least one successful billing cycle on a Professional, Practice, or Enterprise tier. Does not include free-trial users, pilot users who have not been invoiced, or Retail-tier subscribers. A subscription is counted as "paying" only upon receipt of the first payment.

**Why This Is the North Star:**
- Revenue-attributable: directly tied to ARR, the financial signal that the product has monetary value to customers.
- Cohort-trackable: each cohort (Day-0 launch cohort, Day-30 cohort, Day-60 cohort) can be tracked independently, enabling cohort analysis and early churn signal detection.
- Milestone-bound: the 0→1 goal is explicitly defined as 10 paying subscriptions by Day 90 — a binary, unambiguous milestone.
- Non-gamed: cannot be inflated by vanity metrics (followers, signups, trials) that don't translate to revenue. The metric requires real commitment from the customer.

**Day 90 Target:** 10 paying advisor subscriptions

**Phase 2 Signals — When to Scale:**
The following conditions, taken together, indicate readiness to move from solo-founder motion to scaled acquisition (paid LinkedIn ads, content amplification budget, part-time sales hire):

1. **Day-60 trial-to-paid conversion rate >15%** — indicates the product is landing with the advisor segment and the demo is effective. If conversion is <10%, revisit the demo script and trial onboarding before scaling.
2. **At least 3 paying customers acquired through cold channels** (LinkedIn outbound, cold email, or partner referrals — not personal network) — proves the ICP targeting and messaging work without founder warmth.
3. **CAC trending below ₹10,000 and declining** — indicates content and outbound are gaining efficiency. A rising CAC means channels are saturating or messaging is off-target.
4. **Demo-to-pilot conversion rate >35%** — indicates the discovery call is qualifying the right prospects and the demo is closing the sale.

**If all four conditions are met by Day 90:** Signal is positive for Phase 2 scaling. Budget ₹25,000–₹50,000/month for paid LinkedIn ads targeting Indian wealth managers and RIAs, and consider engaging a freelance sales development representative (SDR) for LinkedIn outbound volume in Month 4.

**If three or fewer conditions are met by Day 90:** Stay in Phase 1. Focus on channel-level ROI triage — cut channels below 0.5 trials per 100 outreach touches, double down on the top 2 channels, and run a second 90-day cohort before scaling.

### 8.5 KPI Review Cadence

**Daily (5 minutes — every morning before market open):**
- Check trial activations from the prior day (MRI auth dashboard)
- Check new discovery calls booked (Calendly notifications)
- Log any new paying customers (Stripe dashboard)
- Quick note: "What worked yesterday? What didn't?"

**Weekly (30 minutes — every Monday morning):**
- Full KPI dashboard review: all 7 leading indicators vs. target
- Content output review: were all planned blog posts, LinkedIn posts, and newsletters published on schedule?
- Pipeline review: how many discovery calls are in the CRM? How many are at each stage?
- Week-ahead planning: which content is publishing, which outbound sequences are running, which partners need follow-up?

**Bi-weekly (60 minutes — every other Thursday):**
- Channel-level ROI analysis: calculate trials generated per outreach touch for each channel (LinkedIn organic, LinkedIn outbound, cold email, partner referrals, webinars)
- Identify the top 2 channels by CAC and trial quality — double down on those
- Kill or pause channels delivering <0.2 trials per 100 outreach touches
- Adjust copy and targeting based on engagement data from the past two weeks

**Monthly (90 minutes — first Friday of the month):**
- North Star metric review: how many paying customers? How many in the pipeline?
- ARR and churn review: any cancellations or pauses this month? What was the reason?
- Content effectiveness analysis: which blog posts and LinkedIn posts drove the most trial activations? Double down on those topic formats.
- Partnership pipeline review: any new broker or AMC partnership conversations that need follow-up?
- 90-day forecast update: are we on track for 10 paying customers by Day 90? If not, what needs to change?
