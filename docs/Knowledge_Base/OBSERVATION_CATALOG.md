# CAI Observation Catalog

This document is the master registry of all permissible observations within the CAI ecosystem. 
**Core Rule:** Before a Rule can be written to consume an observation, that observation must first be formally defined in this catalog. This ensures consistent terminology across technical, fundamental, portfolio, and market data.

## 1. Technical Observations
* Weekly Higher High
* Weekly Higher Low
* Breakout Confirmed
* EMA20 Rising
* EMA50 Rising
* Volume Expansion
* ATR Expansion

## 2. Fundamental (Financial) Observations
* Revenue Growth
* EBITDA Growth
* PAT Growth
* Gross Margin
* Operating Margin
* ROCE
* ROE
* Free Cash Flow
* Net Debt

## 3. Fundamental (Management) Observations
* Guidance Raised
* Guidance Maintained
* Guidance Cut
* Capacity Expansion
* Acquisition
* Dividend Policy

## 4. Portfolio Observations
* Position Weight
* Sector Weight
* Cash Available
* Tranche Number

## 5. Market Regime Observations
* Bull Regime
* Bear Regime
* Sector Rotation
* Liquidity Tightening
