# Minervini Research Feasibility

## Objective
Determine whether the core Minervini methodology can be reconstructed sufficiently from the available daily data to perform a research-only historical signal study.

## Methodology
The proxy scan applied the following filters over the period `2024-01-01` to `2025-12-31`:
1. `Price > EMA 50 > EMA 200`
2. `Price > 1.3 * 52W Low`
3. `Price > 0.75 * 52W High`

## Execution Summary
- **Total trading days analyzed:** 519
- **Average number of stocks passing per day:** 214.8
- **Total unique stocks ever passing:** 782
- **Maximum passing on a single day:** 368

## Conclusion
Based on the number of historical signals generated by the proxy Minervini trend template, the core setup produces a meaningful and robust number of candidates. **Proceeding with proper data infrastructure and a full backtest is strongly justified.**
